Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short, honest answers about Loona Automation and its 918 free tools. If your question is not here, email us at loonaautomation@gmail.com.

Is Loona Automation really free?

Yes. All 918 tools across the Loona network are free to use, with no accounts, no subscriptions and no trial that expires. The sites are supported by advertising.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in on Loona tool sites. You open a tool and use it straight away.

What is Loona Automation?

Loona Automation is a network of free online tool websites — calculators, QR code generators, PDF, image, text, study, Islamic, salary, hours and other tools — built and maintained by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer in Pakistan.

How is my data handled?

Most Loona tools run entirely inside your own web browser, so the text, numbers, photos and files you work with are processed on your device and are not sent to us. A small number of AI features send the text you submit to an AI service only to produce your result. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Which languages are the tools available in?

The Loona network is published in 40 language versions — 35 languages plus 5 regional variants — so you can use the tools in the language you searched in.

Who built Loona Automation?

Loona Automation was built by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer from Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan. His mission, called Bolti Duniya, is that useful tools and books should speak to everyone, in every language.

How do I report an error or suggest a new tool?

Email us at loonaautomation@gmail.com or use the Feedback page. Please include the tool name, the page link, what you expected and what actually happened — that helps us reproduce and fix the problem quickly.
