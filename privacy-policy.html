Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Loona Automation (this website, https://loonaautomation.github.io/, and the tools linked from it) handles information.

Information we collect

Our tools are designed to need as little of your information as possible. Most Loona tools run entirely in your own browser; the text, photos or numbers you enter there stay on your device and are not sent to us. Where a feature works through an online AI service, the content you submit is sent to that service only to produce your result.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Cookies and advertising

This site itself sets no tracking cookies. In the future our sites may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Third-party services

Pages may load fonts or libraries from trusted providers such as Google Fonts and cdnjs; those providers may see your IP address as part of delivering the file, as is normal across the web.

Children

Our sites are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For any privacy question, email loonaautomation@gmail.com.
