Featured interview

Aurang Zeb Loona on why online tools should be free

Leaders Perception — Founder interview · Published 22 November 2026

In this interview, our founder explains the idea at the heart of Loona Automation: basic online tools — the calculators, converters, generators and everyday utilities that people need for ordinary tasks — should be free for everyone, with no sign-up wall, no payment demand and no trial that runs out in the middle of the work. He describes how the problem was never the tool itself, but the door placed in front of it: the account form, the card request and the paywall that make people abandon a simple job half done.

The interview also tells his own story. Aurang Zeb Loona is a school teacher in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan, who built Loona Automation after watching the people around him — a bank-officer cousin who needed a simple age calculation every day, a school clerk keeping attendance and marks registers, fellow teachers, and his own students and family — struggle with tools that were locked, complicated or priced for someone else. His mission, which he calls Bolti Duniya ("a speaking world"), is that useful tools and books should speak to everyone, in every language, including the people who cannot read easily. About Loona Automation

Loona Automation is a free online tools network founded in 2026 by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer based in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan. The network offers 918 free online tools across 32 websites — including calculators, PDF tools, text tools, image tools, developer tools, study tools, Islamic tools, salary and tax calculators, and working-hours calculators — published in up to 40 language editions so that people can find and use each tool in their own language.

Every Loona tool follows the same rules: it is completely free, it needs no sign-up or sign-in, there is nothing to download or install, and wherever the technology allows, the work happens inside the visitor's own browser so their files and text stay on their own device. The free tool is the whole tool — not a limited demo of a paid product. Loona is supported by advertising, and its long-term goal is simple: to be the first name people think of when they need one everyday job done quickly, correctly and free.
