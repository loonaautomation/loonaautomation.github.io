About Loona Automation

Loona Automation builds free online tools and practical AI services that solve everyday problems — for students, teachers, mothers, small shopkeepers, freelancers and businesses.

The brand was founded by Aurang Zeb Loona in Pakistan, with a simple belief: useful technology should be within everyone's reach, in the languages people actually speak — English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.

What we make

Free tools anyone can use in a browser — a QR code generator with 30+ code types, an image compressor that hits an exact KB size, AI assistants, teacher toolkits, a browser video editor, expense and habit trackers — and AI services for businesses: an AI office manager, a 24/7 AI calling agent, lead scoring and video ad creation.

Our promise

No fake "free" that expires. No forced signups. No watermarks on your work. Where a tool can run on your own device, it does — your data stays yours.
