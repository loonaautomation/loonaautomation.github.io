Questions people ask

Are Loona tools really free?

Yes. The core tools on this page are free to use — no signup, no watermark and no trial that suddenly expires. The websites are supported by advertising to cover their costs, but the tools stay free.

Is my data private?

Most Loona tools run entirely in your browser — your photos, files and text are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us. Where a tool uses an online AI service, the page says so clearly.
