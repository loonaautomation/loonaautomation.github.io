What Is Loona Automation?

Loona Automation is a small, independent studio from Jaranwala, in Pakistan's Faisalabad district, that builds free online tools and practical AI services. It was founded by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher who also trains other teachers to use artificial intelligence in their classrooms. The tools began as answers to problems met in ordinary working life — a photograph that had to fit an admission form's strict size limit, a zakat calculation a colleague wanted checked, a first proper invoice for a small shopkeeper — and grew, one solved problem at a time, into the collection on this page.

Everything here follows three rules. The tools are free: no accounts, no subscriptions, no trial that expires halfway through your task. They respect your privacy: most Loona tools run entirely inside your web browser, so the photos, documents and figures you work with are processed on your own phone or computer and are never uploaded to us. And they are built for real conditions — modest phones, slow connections, and people who have a job to finish, not an afternoon to spend learning new software. Behind the tools sits the mission described further down this page: Bolti Duniya, the belief that every book should speak, in every language, for everyone who cannot read. The free tools are how we reach people today; that mission is where we are heading.

The Tool Collections, Explained

The cards above are the short version. Here is what each collection actually does, who it is for, and how people use it in real life.

Calculators & Converters

With 292 calculators, Loona Calculators is our largest collection, and it exists because small calculations cause outsized stress when money, exams or deadlines hang on them. Students work out CGPA, percentages and their exact age; families estimate electricity bills, BMI and zakat; borrowers test loan payments before signing anything; and people planning work or travel count the working days of thirty countries, including most of Europe, the United States and Canada. Every calculator answers instantly, and where it matters it shows the working as well, because an unexplained number is hard to trust. Consider a parent checking a PKR 1,000,000 car loan at 24% a year over three years: the calculator shows the monthly payment of about PKR 39,233 — and also the roughly PKR 412,400 of interest hiding behind it, which is the figure that actually changes decisions. Unit converters for length, weight, temperature, speed and more sit alongside the calculators, so a measurement in one system becomes a number you can use in the other.

PDF Tools

The PDF is the format of official life — application forms, contracts, certificates, scanned degrees — and the format people most often need to change without owning expensive software. Loona PDF Tools brings together twenty tools that merge several files into one, split a long document into parts, compress an oversized scan, convert pages to images and back, rotate and reorder pages, fill in forms, sign, and compare two versions of a document side by side to see exactly what changed. The defining choice behind the whole set is privacy: every operation runs inside your browser, so a passport scan or a salary slip never travels to a server and there is nothing to delete afterwards. A teacher combining a class's worksheets into one printable file, or an applicant merging a CNIC copy and a degree scan into the single upload a portal demands, is usually finished in under a minute. Job applicants also use the form filler for the PDF application forms that still arrive without editable fields.

Salary & Tax Calculators

End-of-service money and take-home pay are the two sums workers most need to predict and least want to get wrong. Loona Salary & Tax Calculators covers both, country by country, using the official rules rather than rough estimates: UAE gratuity under Article 51 of the current labour law, Saudi end-of-service benefits, Pakistan salary tax on the current Finance Act 2026 table, and gross-to-net salary for Germany, the Netherlands and Spain that accounts for wage tax, social contributions and the standard deductions. Eighty tools in total also include VAT calculators for thirty European countries, checkers for VAT numbers and IBANs, a currency converter, and fee calculators for people selling through Etsy, eBay and PayPal. A typical visit: an expatriate in Dubai with a basic salary of AED 7,500 and six and a half years of service learns that her gratuity is AED 37,500 — 21 days of basic pay for each of the first five years, then 30 days a year — before she ever sits down with HR, so she can check the company's figure against the law instead of accepting it blind.

Image Tools

Most image problems are really form problems: a portal wants a photograph under 50KB, a visa application wants exact pixel dimensions, a marketplace wants a product photo that is not five megabytes. Loona Image Compressor and its 47 companion tools solve these directly. The headline tool compresses images to an exact target size in kilobytes — not "about smaller", but the size the form demands — and can process up to a hundred photos in a batch and hand them back as a single ZIP file. Around it sit a passport and visa photo maker, resizers, a cropper, format converters between JPG, PNG and WebP, a photo-to-PDF maker, and creative tools such as a colour picker, palette generator and contrast checker for designers. Everything runs on your own device: the photograph of your national identity card is never uploaded anywhere, which is precisely why applicants trust it at midnight before a deadline.

Text Tools

Writers, students, teachers and office workers spend surprising amounts of time wrestling text into shape by hand. The 47 tools on Loona Text Tools take over that wrestling: a word and character counter, case converters, a duplicate-line remover, a tool that compares two texts and highlights the differences, a slug generator for clean web addresses, and a strong password generator. Two tools serve our own home ground — a Roman Urdu to Urdu script converter for people who think in one script and must submit in the other — and two serve study: an image-to-text (OCR) tool that pulls typed words out of a photograph, an EPUB reader for e-books, and a citation generator that formats references in APA, MLA and Chicago styles. A teacher checking that an essay meets its word limit, or a student building a bibliography the night before submission, gets the answer in seconds instead of an evening of manual counting.

Islamic Tools

Loona Islamic Tools gathers the daily references a Muslim household actually reaches for. It leads with accurate prayer times for any city in the world — calculated with the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi method and the Hanafi Asr setting by default, the combination most widely used in Pakistan — along with Qibla direction from your location, today's Hijri date, and a Ramadan timetable for fasting through the holy month. Around those sit a digital tasbih counter, the 99 Names of Allah with meanings, a library of everyday duas, and prepared prayer-time pages for thirty cities. The person this serves best is often a traveller: someone who lands in a city they do not know, needs Maghrib before sunset and the Qibla direction in a hotel room, and finds both in one place without installing anything. Our zakat calculator, on the calculators site, and the step-by-step zakat guide on this page complete the set.

Work Hours & Timesheets

For hourly workers in the United States, the week's most important document is the timesheet — and the gap between hours worked and hours paid is where money quietly disappears. Loona Hours is a family of time card and timesheet calculators that total daily and weekly hours from clock-in and clock-out times, convert minutes into the decimal hours payroll systems use, apply weekly overtime after forty hours, and handle California's daily overtime rules, which are stricter than the federal ones. Dedicated pages cover common pay rates and professions, and comparisons between cities, so a worker can see what the same hours are worth elsewhere. A concrete case: at $18 an hour, a 45.5-hour week is 40 regular hours ($720) plus 5.5 overtime hours at time-and-a-half, $27 each ($148.50) — gross pay of $868.50. If a payslip says less, the worker who has done this arithmetic knows exactly which hours to ask about.

QR Codes & Barcodes

A QR code is a small square that does one job perfectly: it moves information from the physical world into a phone without typing. Loona QR Codes generates 37 kinds of them — website links, WiFi logins, WhatsApp chats, digital visiting cards (vCards), payment requests, map locations, text messages, emails, calendar events and more — plus a barcode generator for product and inventory labels. The same site now also scans QR codes and barcodes — with your camera or from an image — and generates QR codes in bulk for labels and price lists. Two things matter about how it works. The codes are static and are created entirely in your browser: the information is inside the printed pattern itself, with no account, no middleman service and no expiry date, so a code printed on a shop counter today still works in five years. And because nothing is uploaded, a WiFi password typed into the generator never leaves your device. The classic use is the café that prints one code per table so guests join the WiFi with a single tap instead of queueing to ask for the password; our WiFi QR guide on this page walks through it step by step.

Developer Tools

Developers and students lose minutes all day to tiny format chores, and Loona Developer Tools collects 37 of the fixes in one place: a JSON formatter and validator that makes tangled data readable, Base64 and URL encoders and decoders, a UUID generator, hash generators, a JWT decoder for inspecting tokens, a timestamp converter, and an HTML entity encoder. Its newest family opens archives in the browser — ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, TAR.GZ, GZ and even ISO disc images — and converts them to ZIP, so receiving a file in a format your computer cannot open stops being a dead end. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded; the file your client sent as a .rar is opened on your own machine and its contents listed for download. A student on a shared computer, or a freelancer whose laptop has no archive software, gets unstuck without administrator rights or a suspicious download.

How Loona Tools Are Built

Every tool on this network was written for the problem it solves — not copied from a template pack and not generated in bulk. Where a tool depends on a rule, the rule comes from the source that owns it: labour-law articles for gratuity, the Federal Board of Revenue's published tables for Pakistan tax, official minimum-wage orders for pay calculations, and the long-established astronomical conventions behind prayer times. Before a calculator is published, its output is checked against hand-worked examples, and when a government changes a table — a new finance act, a new wage order — the affected tools are updated to the new figures rather than left quietly wrong. Where the honest answer is "this depends on your situation", the page says so and points to the professional or scholar who can decide it.

Privacy is an engineering choice here, not a slogan: if a job can be done inside the visitor's browser, it is done there, and the file never reaches a server. Pages are kept deliberately light so they load quickly on inexpensive phones and slow connections, because that is what much of our audience actually uses. Finally, the tools are corrected in the open — readers report errors by email, fixes go live at the same address, and the guides on this page are expanded whenever a question keeps arriving that a tool alone does not answer.
